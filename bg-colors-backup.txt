    Old palette measured 1.07 : 1 (tile vs crimson) and 1.14 : 1 (tile vs indigo):

    which is what made the tiles read as flat cut-outs / too much contrast.

    rather than a hue clash; white text keeps a ~9.9:1 ratio on the tile.
